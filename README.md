## 🎯 About This Project

Career decisions are among the most important decisions students make. However, reliable, structured and accessible career information is not always easy to find.

Career Roadmaps India aims to make career exploration more accessible by publishing free educational resources for students and young professionals across India.

This repository brings together practical career roadmaps, educational guides, skill-development resources and placement preparation material.

Our objective is simple: help people make more informed career decisions.

## 🧭 How to Use These Resources

1. Identify your current educational stage.
2. Explore relevant career pathways.
3. Understand the qualifications and skills required.
4. Compare available education and employment options.
5. Build a practical learning and preparation plan.

Remember: A career roadmap is a starting point, not a fixed formula. Your interests, abilities, circumstances and evolving opportunities should influence your decisions.
