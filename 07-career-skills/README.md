## How to use this collection

1. Choose a skill relevant to your current goal.
2. Read the corresponding guide and complete its practical activities.
3. Apply the skill in projects, teamwork, presentations, internships or workplace situations.
4. Seek feedback and review your progress.
5. Select another skill to develop as your needs change.

## Who can benefit?

School students, college students, engineering and other undergraduate students, graduates, young professionals and educators.
