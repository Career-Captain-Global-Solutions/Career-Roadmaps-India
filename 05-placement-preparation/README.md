## Suggested preparation sequence

1. Check your college placement rules, eligibility criteria and recruitment calendar.
2. Prepare an accurate resume with projects, internships and skills you can demonstrate.
3. Practise aptitude and role-relevant fundamentals.
4. Rehearse technical and behavioural interview answers.
5. Track applications and learn from feedback.

Recruitment stages vary by employer, role and institution. Not every company uses every assessment stage.
