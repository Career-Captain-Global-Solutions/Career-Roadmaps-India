What this collection covers

Understand the bigger picture.

A concise overview of the themes covered by the published resources in this category.

01 · COLLECTION OVERVIEW

Understand recruitment stages

The published roadmap explains common stages such as eligibility screening, aptitude or technical assessments, group discussions where used, technical interviews and HR or managerial interviews.

02 · COLLECTION OVERVIEW

Build a credible profile

Prepare a clear resume and organise academic work, projects, internships, achievements and skills you can substantiate.

03 · COLLECTION OVERVIEW

Practise assessments and interviews

Prepare aptitude, logical reasoning, role-related fundamentals and structured examples that explain how you approach problems.

04 · COLLECTION OVERVIEW

Follow your college process

Check placement-cell rules, company eligibility, recruitment calendars, application instructions and role-specific requirements. Not every employer uses the same selection process.
