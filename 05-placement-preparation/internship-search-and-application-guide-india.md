# Internship Search and Application Guide for Students in India

**An educational resource by Career Captain Global Solutions**

An internship can help students understand workplace expectations, practise skills and explore a field. The quality of the learning experience matters more than the title alone. Review the work, supervision, safety, duration, location, compensation and written terms before accepting.

## 1. Define what you want to learn

Choose a target field and list:
- Skills you want to practise.
- Tasks you hope to observe or perform.
- Your availability and academic constraints.
- Location, remote-work and travel requirements.
- Whether you need a stipend or academic credit.

## 2. Find opportunities responsibly

Explore:
- Your college placement or internship cell.
- Official employer career pages.
- Faculty, alumni and professional networks.
- Government and institutional internship programmes.
- The AICTE National Internship Portal: https://internship.aicte-india.org/
- National Career Service: https://www.ncs.gov.in/

Check eligibility, deadlines and application instructions on the official listing. The existence of a portal or listing does not guarantee selection.

## 3. Prepare your application

A typical application may require a resume, academic details, portfolio or project links and a short statement of interest. Follow the employer's requested format. Explain why the work is relevant to your learning goals and what evidence you can offer.

## 4. Evaluate an internship before accepting

Ask:
- What tasks will I perform?
- Who will supervise and provide feedback?
- What are the start and end dates and expected hours?
- Is it paid, unpaid or credit-bearing?
- Are travel, equipment or other expenses involved?
- What written offer or agreement will be provided?
- What are the safety, confidentiality and data-handling rules?
- Will I receive a completion letter or certificate, and under what conditions?

Do not rely on verbal promises alone.

## 5. Red flags

Be cautious if an organisation:
- Demands payment to secure an internship or interview.
- Requests passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information.
- Uses unofficial email accounts while claiming to represent a known company.
- Offers unrealistic earnings for vague work.
- Refuses to explain duties, supervision or terms.
- Pressures you to accept immediately.

Verify the organisation through its official website and contact channels. Never pay a recruiter merely to obtain a job or internship.

## 6. Make the internship useful

Set learning goals with your supervisor. Keep a weekly record of tasks, skills and feedback without copying confidential company data. Ask for clarification when needed and request a review near completion.

## 7. Official resources

- AICTE National Internship Portal: https://internship.aicte-india.org/
- National Career Service: https://www.ncs.gov.in/
- Your institution's internship policy and placement office.
