## How to use these resources

1. Identify your current academic stage.
2. Review the pathways and subject combinations available to you.
3. Compare course prerequisites, admission requirements and institution options.
4. Discuss your shortlist with parents, teachers or a qualified career professional.
5. Verify current eligibility and admission information directly with the relevant board or institution.

**Important:** Stream names and subject combinations vary across boards and institutions. A stream choice is not a lifelong career decision; use these guides as a starting point for informed exploration.
