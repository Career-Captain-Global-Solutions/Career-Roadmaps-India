How to plan your study abroad journey

A clear process can help you compare options and avoid making decisions based on a single factor.

  1. Define your academic goal. Identify your intended subject, qualification level, preferred intake and long-term career direction.
  2. Shortlist countries and programmes. Compare course structure, entry requirements, language, recognition and university fit.
  3. Build a complete budget. Include tuition, accommodation, food, transport, insurance, travel, deposits and other costs. Check current exchange rates.
  4. Research scholarships and funding. Check official provider pages for eligibility, application windows, required documents and renewal conditions.
  5. Prepare your application. Follow each university's current instructions for academic records, language tests, statements, references and deadlines.
  6. Verify visa and immigration requirements. Use the official government immigration website for the destination and the correct application route.

Important checks before choosing a country

Do not compare destinations using tuition fees alone. Check the total cost of attendance, programme accreditation or recognition where relevant, internship opportunities, language expectations, accommodation availability, health cover, visa conditions and current post-study rules. Immigration policies and university fees can change; confirm details directly with official sources before paying fees or making travel arrangements.
