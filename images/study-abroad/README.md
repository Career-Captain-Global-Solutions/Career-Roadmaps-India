## Asset Guidelines

- Use accurate, student-friendly educational visuals.
- Verify country-specific information using official sources.
- Clearly distinguish general guidance from legal or immigration advice.
- Avoid presenting visa approval, admission or scholarships as guaranteed.
- Maintain consistent Career Captain branding.
- Use the original approved Career Captain logo without recreating or modifying it.
- Optimise images for web performance.
- Verify licensing and attribution requirements.
