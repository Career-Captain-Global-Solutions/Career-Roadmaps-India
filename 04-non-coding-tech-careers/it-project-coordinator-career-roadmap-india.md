# IT Project Coordinator Career Roadmap in India: Responsibilities and Skills

**An educational resource by Career Captain Global Solutions**

IT project coordinators support the planning, communication and tracking needed to deliver technology projects. They help teams stay organised, surface dependencies and maintain project information. The role is not identical to project management; authority and accountability vary by organisation.

## 1. Common responsibilities

- Maintain schedules, action logs and project documentation.
- Arrange meetings and record decisions.
- Track tasks, milestones, risks and dependencies.
- Follow up with owners on agreed actions.
- Coordinate communication across business and technical teams.
- Help prepare status reports.
- Escalate blockers through agreed channels.
- Support change and handover documentation.

## 2. Skills employers may value

- Organisation, prioritisation and attention to detail.
- Clear written and verbal communication.
- Meeting facilitation and follow-through.
- Spreadsheet and presentation skills.
- Familiarity with task trackers and collaboration platforms.
- Basic understanding of software development and IT delivery.
- Risk, issue and dependency tracking.
- Professional judgement when handling confidential information.

## 3. Understand delivery approaches

Projects may use predictive, iterative, Agile or hybrid approaches. Learn the purpose of planning, backlog management, stand-ups, reviews, retrospectives, milestones and change control. Do not assume every company follows the same framework.

## 4. Build evidence

Create a sample project pack for a clearly labelled hypothetical project:
- Scope and objectives.
- Stakeholder list.
- Milestone schedule.
- Responsibility matrix.
- Risk and issue log.
- Meeting notes and action tracker.
- Weekly status report.

This demonstrates organisation and communication without claiming real project delivery experience.

## 5. Entry pathways

Relevant backgrounds may include business, engineering, IT, commerce and administration. Potential starting roles include project coordinator, PMO analyst, delivery support, implementation coordinator or operations associate. Employer expectations differ.

## 6. Interview preparation

Prepare examples of how you organise competing tasks, communicate a delay, document a decision, follow up on an action and handle conflicting priorities. Explain what you did and the result without exaggeration.

## 7. Six-week preparation plan

Weeks 1–2: Learn project lifecycle concepts and review role descriptions.

Weeks 3–4: Practise schedules, trackers, meeting notes and status reporting.

Weeks 5–6: Build a sample project pack, update your CV and rehearse scenario questions.

Research current openings through employer career pages and https://www.ncs.gov.in/.
